The brief

EMS brings electronics, clothing, pharmacy and supershop workflows into one practical, dependable retail platform. The approved provider line is Powered by Dexter Studio and stays separate from the logo lockup.

Final design

Shared Core

Four modular corner forms link diagonally into one shared core, joining distinct shop workflows under one platform.

  • The four outer modules echo EMS's different shop types; the central hub signals a shared operational core.
  • Rounded outer corners and diagonal links soften the first sketch's focus-frame reading.
  • A two-colour teal palette stays practical in print, while one-colour and reverse versions preserve the form.
