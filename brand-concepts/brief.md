# EMS identity refresh — final brief

## Brand
- **Name:** EMS.
- **Product:** a multi-shop retail management system spanning electronics, clothing, pharmacy and supershop operations.
- **Audience:** business owners, administrators and store staff who need dependable day-to-day control.
- **Brand cues:** clear, capable, practical, connected.
- **Provider line:** **Powered by Dexter Studio**, kept separate from the core logo so the EMS mark can stand alone.

## Chosen direction — B: Shared Core
Four modular corners and diagonal links converge on a rounded-diamond hub. The four corners represent different shop workflows on one shared platform; the small-size cut thickens the modules and hub for icon use. Optical adjustments soften the outer corners and give the inner links a distinctive 45-degree approach, reducing the literal scan-frame reading from the first sketch.

## Palette
- Evergreen teal `#0F766E` — primary mark, UI and app-icon tile.
- Core teal `#0D9488` — shared hub accent.
- Deep ink `#132A27` — wordmark and headings.
- Soft mint `#F4F8F7` — light surfaces and splash backgrounds.

## Delivery
The production masters, outlined wordmark, horizontal and stacked lockups, color/mono/reverse variants, favicon/PWA exports, splash screens, landing illustration and usage guide are in `assets/brand/` and `assets/`.
The original A/B/C sheet remains an exploration record, not a production asset.
