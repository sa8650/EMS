# EMS V1 · Premium Add-Ons User Guide

This guide explains how administrators purchase and use Premium Add-Ons in EMS V1.

> **Premium Add-Ons are optional paid services.** They are separate from normal EMS license plans and do not change your shop-create limit or normal license expiry date.

---

## 1. Available Add-Ons

| Add-On | What it does | Daily limit means |
|---|---|---|
| **ConnectX** | Lets a shop send business emails through EMS. | Maximum emails per day for each shop. |
| **Zudo AI** | Gives the shop access to the read-only Zudo AI assistant. | Maximum AI requests per day for each shop. |
| **AI Business Health** | Creates a business-health report using selected business data. | Maximum health-report requests per day for each shop. |

The EMS Owner decides which Add-Ons are available, pricing, allowed number of days, and allowed daily limits.

---

## 2. Who Can Purchase an Add-On?

Only an **Administrator** can purchase Premium Add-Ons.

Shop staff cannot purchase an Add-On. Shop staff may use an approved Add-On only if their administrator has purchased it and their staff permission allows access to that module.

---

## 3. Open Premium Add-Ons

1. Sign in as an **Administrator**.
2. In the left menu, select **Premium Add-Ons**.
3. You will see available Add-On cards:
   - ConnectX
   - Zudo AI
   - AI Business Health

Each card shows its name, description, and current availability.

---

## 4. Understand Add-On Card Status

| Card status | Meaning | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Add to cart** | The Add-On is available for purchase. | Choose it and add it to your cart. |
| **In cart** | You already selected it for the current checkout. | Complete checkout or remove it from cart. |
| **Pending** | A payment request was submitted and is waiting for EMS Owner review. | Wait for approval or rejection. |
| **Active** | Your Add-On is approved and still valid. | Use the feature until its expiry date. |
| **Included** | This feature is already included in your active normal license. | No extra purchase is needed. |
| **Inactive / disabled** | The EMS Owner has not enabled this Add-On for sale. | It cannot be purchased now. |

---

## 5. Add an Item to Cart

1. Click **Add to cart** on the desired Add-On.
2. A selection window opens.
3. Select:
   - **Validity days** — how long you want the Add-On.
   - **Daily limit** — email limit for ConnectX, or AI request limit for Zudo / AI Business Health.
4. EMS calculates and shows the **Total** automatically.
5. Click **Add to cart**.

The selection window closes and the Checkout section opens on the right side.

### Price calculation

The price is calculated as:

```text
Validity days × Daily limit × Owner unit price
```

Example:

```text
30 days × 10 AI requests per day × 2 BDT = 600 BDT
```

The price and allowed minimum/maximum values are controlled by EMS Owner.

---

## 6. Use Checkout

The Checkout section appears only when at least one Add-On is in your cart.

It shows:

- Selected Add-On name
- Daily email/request limit
- Validity days
- Product total
- Delete button
- Subtotal
- Coupon field
- Coupon discount
- Grand Total
- Payment method
- Payment number
- Transaction ID

### Remove an Add-On

Click the **×** delete button beside a cart item.

- The total updates automatically.
- If you remove the last item, the Checkout section closes automatically.

---

## 7. Apply a Coupon

1. Enter the coupon code in the Coupon box.
2. Click **Apply**.
3. If the coupon is valid and active, EMS immediately recalculates:
   - Coupon discount
   - Grand Total

If the code is invalid, inactive, or expired, EMS shows an error and no discount is applied.

Coupons apply only to **Premium Add-On Checkout**. They do not apply to normal EMS License purchases.

---

## 8. Complete Payment and Submit Checkout

Before confirming, you must provide all three details:

1. **Payment method** — bKash or Nagad
2. **Payment number**
3. **Transaction ID**

Click **Confirm purchase** only after you complete payment through the payment method provided by EMS Owner.

EMS will not submit checkout if:

- The cart is empty
- Payment method is missing
- Payment number is missing
- Transaction ID is missing
- Selected days or daily limit are outside the allowed range

After a successful request, the status becomes **Pending**.

> Payment requests are manually reviewed by EMS Owner. A submitted request does not activate the Add-On immediately.

---

## 9. Add-On Purchase History

The bottom of the Premium Add-Ons page shows your purchase history.

You can see:

- Add-On name
- Validity days
- Daily limit
- Amount
- Payment method
- Transaction ID
- Status
- Expiry date after approval

### Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for EMS Owner review. |
| **Active** | Approved and available for use. |
| **Rejected** | Payment request was rejected. Contact EMS Owner if you need details. |
| **Expired** | The approved Add-On period has ended. You may purchase/renew it again. |

---

## 10. Rules for Active Add-Ons

### A. Cannot buy the same Add-On twice while active

If an Add-On is active, its card remains disabled until it expires.

After expiry, you can purchase it again as a renewal.

### B. License-included feature rule

If your active normal EMS license already includes a feature, that matching Add-On is marked **Included**.

Example:

- Your license includes Zudo AI
- The Zudo AI Add-On card shows **Included**
- You do not need to purchase Zudo AI separately
- You may still purchase ConnectX or AI Business Health if they are not included in your license

### C. Add-Ons do not increase store limit

Premium Add-Ons do **not** create more shops and do not change your license store capacity.

Example:

- Your license allows 2 shops
- Purchasing Zudo AI does not change the limit to 3 shops

### D. Add-Ons do not extend normal license expiry

Premium Add-On validity is separate from your normal EMS license validity.

---

## 11. Read-Only Shop Rule

If a shop is Read-Only because of normal license capacity or license expiry, an approved Add-On can still be used only for its own purpose.

| Add-On | Allowed in Read-Only shop |
|---|---|
| ConnectX | Send allowed business emails within its daily limit. |
| Zudo AI | Ask permitted Zudo AI questions within its daily limit. |
| AI Business Health | Generate permitted business health reports within its daily limit. |

Purchasing an Add-On does **not** unlock normal editing actions in a Read-Only shop.

It does not allow:

- New sales invoices
- New purchase invoices
- Inventory edits
- Expense edits
- Customer edits
- Supplier edits
- Staff edits
- Shop capacity changes

---

## 12. Daily Limit Rule

Every approved Add-On has a daily limit.

Examples:

```text
ConnectX: 30 emails/day
Zudo AI: 10 requests/day
AI Business Health: 2 reports/day
```

When the daily limit is reached, wait until the next day to use the Add-On again.

The limit resets daily. The Add-On remains available until its expiry date.

---

## 13. Need Help?

Contact your EMS Owner/administrator support contact when:

- A payment request remains Pending for a long time
- A payment request is Rejected
- Your transaction ID was entered incorrectly
- You believe your Add-On was approved but still shows inactive
- You need a larger daily email/request limit
- You need to renew an expired Add-On

Do not submit duplicate payment requests for an Add-On that is already Pending or Active.
