# EMS License Rules Book

**Product:** EMS Multi-Store Management System (EMS)  
**Purpose:** The key rules for understanding licenses and Premium Add-Ons, troubleshooting common problems, and supporting users accurately.

This handbook is divided into three parts: **Users & Administrators**, **Platform Owner**, and **AI Support Agents**. Always trust the current EMS purchase record, status, dates, plan details, and displayed error over assumptions.

---

# PART 1 — USERS & BUSINESS ADMINISTRATORS

## 1. How an EMS License Works

1. A license belongs to a Business Administrator account and defines the plan, validity dates, shop capacity, included features, and limits.
2. The currently effective license determines access. **Separate license purchases do not add their shop limits together.**
3. A plan may include features such as ConnectX, Zudo AI, AI Business Health, TrueBill, or Vaultium. Always check the selected plan for the exact included features and limits.
4. Paying for a plan does not activate it automatically. A paid purchase must be reviewed and approved by the EMS Owner.
5. An eligible ৳0 plan may activate automatically, subject to the eligibility rules shown by EMS.
6. A valid license does not override shop status, staff permissions, feature limits, Add-On status, or a service-provider/configuration problem.
7. Premium Add-Ons are separate from the main license. They do not add shop capacity or extend the main license's expiry date.

## 2. License Statuses

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | The purchase is waiting for Owner review. | Check purchase history and wait for a decision. Do not pay again or submit a duplicate request. |
| **Active** | The purchase was approved. | Check its start date and expiry date. It is usable only during its valid period. |
| **Rejected** | The Owner rejected the purchase claim. | Read the review note, if available, and contact HelpDesk if unclear. Do not assume a refund is automatic. |
| **Expired / no valid license** | No license is currently valid for normal operation. | Select an available plan and complete the purchase/approval process. Shops may be placed in Read-Only mode. |
| **Future start date** | The approved license period has not started yet. | Check the displayed date and whether another license is currently effective. |

**Where to check:** Open **Licenses → My License Purchases** for license status and dates; **Premium Add-Ons** for Add-On status/history; **Store Manage → Shop Capacity** for shop status and capacity; and **HelpDesk** for support.

## 3. Buying a License

1. Open **Licenses** and compare the plan's price, duration, shop allowance, included features, and limits.
2. Select an available plan. An eligible free plan may activate automatically.
3. For a paid plan, follow the payment instructions shown in EMS and use the displayed supported payment method, such as bKash or Nagad.
4. Enter the required payment details accurately, including the transaction ID, and submit the claim once.
5. Check **My License Purchases** and wait for the Owner's decision.
6. After approval, check the effective start date, expiry date, plan entitlements, and shop allowance.

### What if I cannot purchase a license?

- **Plan is unavailable:** It may have been disabled or removed. Choose a plan that is currently offered or contact HelpDesk.
- **Required payment information is missing:** Complete the fields shown by EMS and check for typing errors.
- **Transaction ID is already used:** Do not reuse it or immediately pay again. Check purchase history and contact HelpDesk if the earlier request is unclear.
- **Free plan is blocked:** The account may not be eligible, may have used the free plan before, or may already have a valid license.
- **I am Shop Staff:** License purchases are handled by the Business Administrator.
- **The page shows an unexplained error:** Save the exact message and contact HelpDesk. Do not keep submitting the same purchase.

## 4. Upgrade, Downgrade, Renewal, and Multiple Licenses

### Upgrade to a higher plan

1. Compare the new plan's capacity, duration, included features, and limits.
2. Submit one purchase request and wait for Owner approval if payment is required.
3. After approval, verify which license is effective and check its dates and entitlements.
4. If capacity increases, use **Store Manage → Shop Capacity** to activate eligible shops within the new allowance.

**What if I upgraded but the old plan's time is left?** Do not assume the unused time transfers, extends the new plan, or becomes a refund/credit. Qualifying plan changes may take effect immediately after approval. Check the actual dates shown in EMS; ask the Owner about any account-specific discrepancy.

### Downgrade to a lower plan

1. Before purchasing, check whether the lower plan has enough shop capacity, features, storage, and usage limits.
2. If the lower allowance cannot cover all current shops, some shops may need to become Read-Only or Inactive.
3. Confirm that the business can operate without any features or limits removed by the new plan.
4. After approval, review the effective dates, entitlements, and shop statuses.

**What if a feature or shop stops working after downgrade?** Check whether the new plan includes that feature and whether shop capacity was reduced. Do not assume the old plan remains in force until its original expiry date. No unused-time refund or credit should be assumed.

### Renew the same plan

Submit a renewal request and check its status. An approved same-plan renewal may start after the current valid period ends; if the previous license has already expired, the new period may take effect from approval. Always use the actual start and expiry dates shown by EMS.

### Buy multiple licenses

- Their shop limits do **not** stack. The effective license controls capacity and entitlements.
- Multiple purchase records can have different statuses or effective dates. Do not create extra purchases to try to combine limits.
- Submit one request at a time and check the result before submitting another.
- If several claims are already Pending, stop creating new claims and ask HelpDesk to have the Owner review them.
- Do not assume licenses can transfer between Administrator accounts.

**What if I bought the wrong plan or submitted twice?** Do not submit another payment. Check the purchase history and contact HelpDesk with the relevant record(s). The Owner must review the case; a refund, cancellation, or transfer is not automatic.

## 5. Shop Capacity, Expiry, and Read-Only Mode

- **Active shop:** Normal operation is allowed when the license, module entitlement, service availability, and staff permissions also allow it.
- **Read-Only shop:** Existing records can be viewed, but normal business changes are restricted. Buying an Add-On does not remove these restrictions.
- **Inactive shop:** The shop is unavailable for normal operation.
- If the plan's shop allowance is reached, EMS may prevent creating or activating another shop. Check **Shop Capacity** and the current plan limit.
- If a license expires or there is no valid license, normal shop changes may be restricted. Choose an available plan and complete activation/renewal.
- **Do not permanently delete a shop just to free capacity unless its business data is no longer needed.** Permanent deletion can remove that shop's data.

**What if my license says Active but my shop is Read-Only?** Check start/expiry dates, the currently effective plan, shop status, shop capacity, and whether a plan change took effect. Refresh or sign in again once. If the record is valid and the problem remains, send the details to HelpDesk rather than paying again.

## 6. Premium Add-Ons

### Purchase and validity rules

1. Check **Premium Add-Ons** to see which Add-Ons are offered and whether the current license already includes the feature.
2. If a feature is already included in the current plan, do not buy its Add-On separately unless EMS explicitly says it is required.
3. For a paid Add-On, submit the displayed payment details once and wait for Owner approval. Payment alone does not activate it.
4. Check the Add-On's status, start/validity period, expiry, and daily or usage limit.
5. Add-On validity is separate from main-license validity. An Add-On does not extend the license or add shop capacity.
6. An active Add-On generally cannot be purchased again until it expires. Availability and renewal depend on the options shown in EMS.
7. Reaching a daily limit usually means waiting for its reset or asking the Owner whether a different plan/limit is available.

### What if I cannot buy an Add-On?

- **Already active:** Check its expiry and limit; it may not be purchasable again until it expires.
- **Pending:** Wait for the Owner's decision; do not pay again.
- **Included in the current plan:** Check the included entitlement instead of buying it separately.
- **Disabled or unavailable:** The Owner controls Add-On availability.
- **Payment or transaction error:** Verify the displayed details and purchase history before taking another step.
- **No clear reason:** Save the exact error and contact HelpDesk.

### What if I bought an Add-On but it does not work?

Check in this order:

1. Confirm the correct Add-On purchase, status, dates, expiry, and limit in Add-On history.
2. If Pending, it is still awaiting review. If Rejected, check the review note and contact the Owner.
3. If Expired, check whether renewal is available.
4. If Active, confirm the correct shop is selected, the shop is not Inactive, and your staff account has the needed permission.
5. Check whether the usage limit has been reached. Read the exact error message.
6. If it still fails, contact HelpDesk. The Owner may need to check service configuration or provider availability.

Do not purchase the same Add-On again just because the feature fails. An approved Add-On does not guarantee that an external provider or the feature's configuration is working correctly.

## 7. Getting Help: What to Send

Contact **HelpDesk** and include only what is needed to investigate: Administrator/shop name or ID, plan or Add-On name, displayed status and dates, exact error message, transaction reference when relevant, and approximately when the issue happened. **Never send a password, OTP, payment PIN, or secret key.**

# PART 2 — PLATFORM OWNER

## 1. Owner Responsibilities

The Owner is responsible for plan and Add-On availability, pricing and limits, reviewing payment claims, making approval decisions, resolving entitlement/configuration issues, and handling HelpDesk escalations.

## 2. License and Add-On Administration Rules

1. Keep plan details accurate: price, duration, shop allowance, feature entitlements, storage, and usage limits.
2. Keep Add-On details accurate: price, validity, daily/usage limits, included-plan rules, and availability.
3. Review a payment claim against its submitted method, transaction reference, amount, selected plan/Add-On, and purchase history before approving or rejecting it.
4. Do not approve an unclear or duplicate claim without checking the existing record. Leave a clear reason when rejecting if the system supports a review note.
5. After approval, confirm the purchase record and effective dates reflect the intended result.
6. A plan entitlement, shop status, staff permission, and provider configuration are separate checks. Fix the correct layer rather than selling another plan/Add-On as a workaround.
7. Do not promise refunds, prorated credits, unused-time carry-over, plan transfers, or special stacking unless an explicit Owner policy and supported process authorize it.

## 3. What If? Owner Decision Checklist

- **Payment received but transaction cannot be verified:** Leave the request unresolved until evidence is checked. Ask for the correct transaction reference through HelpDesk; do not approve based on a screenshot alone if it does not establish the payment.
- **Duplicate or multiple license requests:** Review all related records and their order. Clarify which plan should be effective and communicate the decision. Do not add the shop limits together.
- **Upgrade or downgrade approved but result is unexpected:** Check the effective license, start/expiry dates, entitlement values, and shop capacity. Explain the actual result. Do not promise unused-time credit unless an approved policy supports it.
- **User cannot buy a plan/Add-On:** Check that it is available, account eligibility, existing Active/Pending records, duplicate transaction references, and required payment fields.
- **Add-On is approved but does not work:** Verify Add-On validity and limit, feature entitlement, selected shop, staff permission, and any required provider/configuration settings. Record the exact failure and resolve or escalate the service issue.
- **License is valid but shops remain Read-Only:** Check current effective dates, account entitlement, active shop count, shop status, and whether the recent decision was applied. If all are correct, investigate the access/activation issue rather than asking the user to pay again.
- **Plan limit is exceeded:** Explain the available options and their consequences. Do not recommend permanent deletion without a clear warning that shop data may be removed.
- **User requests a refund, cancellation, transfer, or exception:** Follow the published Owner policy and record the decision. If no policy/process exists, do not promise the request can be completed.
- **Owner cannot reproduce the problem:** Ask for the exact status/error, relevant purchase reference, affected shop, and approximate time. Avoid collecting passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, or unnecessary sensitive data.

## 4. Owner Communication Rules

Give the user a clear status, the reason for the decision, the next step, and whether any action is required from them. Distinguish **Pending**, **Approved/Active**, **Rejected**, and **Expired**. Do not state that a license or Add-On is activated until its record and effective dates confirm it. Explain any reduction in shop capacity or entitlements after a downgrade.

# PART 3 — AI SUPPORT AGENTS

## 1. Required Handling Sequence

For every licensing question, the AI agent must:

1. **Identify the role and problem:** Business Administrator or Shop Staff; main license, shop capacity, or specific Add-On.
2. **Get the visible evidence:** Ask for the exact status, start/expiry date if shown, affected shop, and exact error message. Do not guess at the user's account state.
3. **Check the correct record:** License issues use **My License Purchases**; Add-On issues use **Premium Add-Ons history**; shop issues use **Shop Capacity**.
4. **Apply the matching rule in Part 1:** Explain what the status means in plain language.
5. **Give one safe next action:** Check dates, wait for Owner review, inspect capacity/permissions/limits, refresh once, or contact HelpDesk.
6. **Escalate Owner-only actions:** Payment decisions, refunds, plan/catalogue changes, account unlocking, provider configuration, or unresolved failures where the record looks correct.
7. **Close accurately:** Say what is confirmed, what remains unknown, and what information the Owner needs. Never claim a fix was completed without confirmation.

## 2. AI What-If Decision Rules

- **“I paid, but it is not active.”** Check status. Pending means waiting for review; Rejected means check the review note; Active still requires valid dates. Do not ask for another payment first.
- **“My license says Active, but my shop is Read-Only.”** Check dates, currently effective plan, shop capacity/status, and recent plan changes. Refresh/sign in again once; if still unresolved, escalate.
- **“I cannot add or activate a shop.”** Compare active shop count with the current plan allowance and inspect the shop status. Do not advise buying another license until capacity and the effective license are verified.
- **“I upgraded/downgraded, but something changed.”** Check the newly effective plan, dates, capacity, and entitlements. Explain reduced features or capacity where applicable; do not invent a credit/refund or say the previous plan must remain effective.
- **“I bought multiple licenses. Do the limits combine?”** No. The currently effective license controls. If there are multiple pending/approved records, escalate to the Owner to clarify the effective order.
- **“I cannot buy an Add-On.”** Check whether it is already Active, Pending, included in the plan, expired, or disabled. Check purchase history before telling the user to retry.
- **“My Add-On is Active but fails.”** Check dates/limits, selected shop, shop status, staff permissions, exact error, and possible provider/configuration issue. Escalate if unresolved; do not tell the user to repurchase it.
- **“The Owner approved it, but the screen did not change.”** Verify the actual purchase record and effective dates; refresh/sign in again once. If the problem remains, escalate with details.
- **“I want a refund or unused time back.”** Explain that it is not automatic. Refer the request to the Owner; do not promise an outcome.
- **“Can I delete a shop to free capacity?”** Explain that permanent deletion can remove business data. Recommend reviewing Read-Only/Inactive options and capacity first.

## 3. Non-Negotiable AI Rules

- Never treat a payment, receipt, cart item, or Pending request as an activated license/Add-On.
- Never ask the user to pay twice, reuse a transaction ID, or create a duplicate claim before checking existing records.
- Never say license shop limits stack, an Add-On increases shop capacity, or an Add-On extends the main license expiry.
- Never promise refunds, proration, unused-time carry-over, automatic transfer, approval, or plan restoration unless the Owner's confirmed policy and record support it.
- Never confuse plan entitlement with shop status, staff permissions, usage limits, or provider availability.
- Never ask for or repeat passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, private keys, or secret credentials.
- Never fabricate prices, limits, dates, status, rejection reasons, or actions performed in an account.
- Never claim to have accessed a live account, database, or Owner console unless the agent actually has authorized access and has checked it.
- If the rules or available records do not settle the issue, state that clearly and escalate to the Owner through HelpDesk.

## 4. Minimum Escalation Details

Send only the information needed: Administrator/shop name or ID, plan/Add-On name, displayed status and dates, exact error, transaction reference when relevant, and approximate time. Exclude passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, and secret keys.

---

**Core rule for all three audiences:** Verify the current record and effective dates, do not duplicate a payment or purchase to troubleshoot, and escalate any decision or system change that only the EMS Owner can authorize.
